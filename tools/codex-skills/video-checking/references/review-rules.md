# Source-fidelity rules

Apply supplied requirements without inventing stricter action or continuity demands.

## Observable picture

- Follow authored actions from reference start state to required endpoint. A source-
  supported cutaway/ellipsis need not show every intermediate movement. Require
  continuous visibility only when the brief actually needs it.
- Separate visible cast/props from preserved offscreen state. A knife or phone
  outside the crop has not been proven lost; a hidden mouth has not been proven
  physically closed. Record that observation boundary.
- Check identity/costume, material setting, hand/prop ownership and unowned events.
  Harmless framing/head motion/background traffic or a small timing shift is not
  automatically material. State the story impact of a failure.
- Distinguish action-start images from completed poses. Do not demand a new movement
  from a finished pose unless that interval actually requires it.
- Small text, hand interactions and mouth states require readable native evidence.
  Expand around uncertainty instead of treating an unclear thumbnail as a pass
  or as definite failure.

## Writing and speech

- Preserve intended physical writing/phone text when required. Distinguish it from
  unowned external subtitles, speech bubbles, labels or graphic inserts. Intended
  text is not a caption defect merely because it is text.
- Exact writing claims require actual readable pixels. Overlay wording is not proof
  that those words were audibly spoken.
- Public speakers may move their mouths. Private voiceover follows its authored
  picture policy: visible lips should comply; eye-only coverage supports mouth
  out of picture, never unseen closure or acoustic privacy certification.
- Missing ASR recognition is not proof of omitted speech. Near-sound/homophone
  spellings are not automatically spoken errors. A material unresolved clause
  needs review unless an independent picture defect already supports failure.
- Supplemental recognition is a targeted evidence request, not an indefinite
  recognizer loop. Preserve primary output; do not feed required lines into ASR.
- A narrow owner listening observation supports only its stated phrase/window,
  not whole-clip approval. Without actual listening, voice identity, acting,
  foley balance and lip sync remain ungraded.

## Reuse and honest claims

Confirm contact-sheet dimensions match declared frame coverage before relying on it;
a mismatch is a preparation issue, not a media-quality rejection.

Reuse content-bound technical evidence; avoid repeating an unchanged full inspection
solely for a new manager. A source fix does not automatically invalidate a usable
older take: compare its actual output against current requirements first.

Attribute reused observations. Never present another reviewer's inspection as your
own. Keep source defects, generated defects, tool/input errors and review mistakes
separate. This optional review does not create a universal production gate or
new dispatch authority.
