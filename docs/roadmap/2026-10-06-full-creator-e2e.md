## Repairs and stopping conditions

For every issue, retain the failure and state what failed, why, supporting
evidence and the correct ownership layer before editing. Fix the contract,
add a focused regression, rerun the original visible action and neighbors.
After two unsuccessful attempts at one criterion, reassess cause/method; do
not repeatedly generate or lower admission to obtain a green result.

Stop when the scoped live journey, complementary coverage, repairs and final
verification are complete; at the hard deadline; or when all remaining work
needs unavailable access/new authority. One blocked branch does not stop other
coverage. Host sleep, sign-in or OS authorization prompts are limitations, not
permission to alter power/security policy. Leave clear resume instructions.

The final receipt must name completed routes/stages, live versus fixture
coverage, failures/fixes, source/static/runtime identities, checks and terminal
results, independent review, actual submissions and any measured usage, owner
preservation, pending CI and exact remaining functional or sensory gaps. A
complete UI journey is not final-film acceptance.
