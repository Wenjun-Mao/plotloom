# ADR 0185: Closed installed-text prefix for explicit image tests

Status: Accepted for bounded local retention, October 17, 2026, after Root and
independent source/correctness review and one separately released matched screen.
Source parent: `6f2af2506f5c0970235cb1f1e1bcc354fca23c80`.
[Scope, evidence and limits](../roadmap/2026-10-17-installed-text-prefix.md).

## Problem and decision

Sixteen explicitly selected image/geometry boundary cases repeat the same real
eight-stage text preparation and production installation. Reuse only that closed
default prefix once per pytest session, through an explicit fixture and factory.
No product runtime changes or global/autouse replacement.

Build through the natural `environment` and unchanged `run_text`. Stop at its return
immediately after installation: current running/references, the existing focused
zero early-reference policy, eight successful terminal text attempts, adopted
source/text/Bridge/installation checkpoints and decisions. Keep original project,
run, policy, request, attempt, receipt and source/Bridge history identities.
Reject custom Brief/policy, unresolved/reserved work, image/reference/inventory
checkpoints, images, geometry, frames, bundles, handoff and human review facts.
The retained creative-evidence owner must validate the exact eight completed
packages, inputs, candidates, reports and completion manifests against their
original attempts/receipts; no restored-omission allowance or unexported success.

Close project handles and service/thread/controller descriptor before freezing.
Reuse `ClosedAutopilotSeed` and its physical file hashes, independent copies,
no-link/no-sidecar checks and exclusive OS ownership locks. No new clone/rebase
system. Each destination receives fresh registry, installed profile/pins, fake
host, service and controller claim through the same factored runtime setup as
natural environments. Invoke ordinary `claim()` and the following fresh
`current()`; retain both actual returns, comparing durable fields and explicit
UTC timestamp meaning across SQLite's aware/naive round trip. Never rewrite
claims, policy, receipts or evidence to manufacture eligibility. New host text
calls/statuses/pending state start honestly empty, rather than fabricating eight.

Only image delivery/geometry knobs apply after the cut. Every reference/image,
original-ready boundary, geometry, restart, rollback, stop and UNKNOWN path stays
real and destination-local. Keep the natural complete-pack test (eight text,
29 images, 27 frames, two routes) AST-identical and on its natural path. Custom
Cast/Art, budgets, portraits, compact packs, early references, writer death and
terminal lineage remain on their existing natural or independently owned seeds.

## Alternatives, consequences and guardrails

Reject caching authority/model objects, copying host/service/controller state,
manual reconstruction of installation, policy rebasing, broad fallback inputs,
and reuse of an image/geometry or completed material pack. The session prefix is
test-owned retained evidence, not creative/product acceptance or a runtime API.

Before converting consumers, prove two independent claims/copies through fresh
image attempts/receipts and geometry lineage, no text resend, rollback/mutation
isolation and unchanged source bytes. Refuse active owners, unresolved history,
nondefault inputs and corrupted retained package/delivery bytes using existing
owners. Run the complete three changed test files plus proportional fixture,
claim, authority, safety, seed and ownership regressions. Preserve failed guard
attempts and diagnoses. Register exact test/helper ownership.

Root accepted 145 distinct focused passing cases and the single control-first,
time-separated screen. Its candidate clock includes natural session-prefix
construction, copying and retained-byte validation. The 16 screen cases overlap
the correctness set. Correctness timings establish no speedup; the separate
screen supports bounded fixture retention subject to order, cache/file-read
history and host-load limits. It establishes no causal/statistical speedup or
current whole-suite/default-two-worker qualification; the 85-percent complete-suite
target remains open. Only documentation closeout and a normal local commit of
the original 12 paths are released. Further runtime, full/whole gates, providers,
CI, push/merge and product/creative acceptance remain separate and held.
