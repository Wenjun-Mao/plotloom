## Post-run direction review · 2026-11-14

**Judgment:** Group 4 achieved the agreed bounded comparison and delivery. It supplies a tentative caption intervention, with no dependable remedy or production default established. This review reuses the fixed observations and completed acceptance evidence. A fresh GPT-6.1 Terra/high reader independently challenged the interpretation; no new media review or generation was needed.

The creator's supplied transcript connects excessive prompt information to captions and recommends deleting unimportant details ([lines 49–51](/Users/wjmao/Downloads/H3_中文校对全文.txt:49), [line 63](/Users/wjmao/Downloads/H3_中文校对全文.txt:63)). It also connects complex actions and dialogue relationships to characters speaking incorrectly and recommends replanning the dialogue ([lines 131–139](/Users/wjmao/Downloads/H3_中文校对全文.txt:131)). The caption test therefore addresses advice in that transcript. It does not evaluate the speech-gibberish rationale.

Our short prompts removed selected descriptions while keeping the same people, intended words and shot structure. Their negative caption result applies to those particular edits. It does not show that reducing dialogue/action complexity is ineffective, or establish how prompt length affects gibberish. Both current and template prompts were English; the creator's Chinese-to-English switch was not tested. Resolution was held fixed, so the transcript's [resolution advice](/Users/wjmao/Downloads/H3_中文校对全文.txt:47) also remains untested.

The decisive Group 4 evidence is the matched confession pattern: all four versions captioned on round 1, all four clear on round 2, and only the two current-format versions captioned on round 3. The template's two gains thus come from one independent seed, represented at two detail levels. For current full → template full, only two of the twelve sets had a captioned current-full control; the template cleared one and remained captioned on the other. Ten both-clear sets provide no observed removal gain. These small, selected counts cannot establish a general success rate.

Template short also adds captions relative to both current short and template full on the first masked seed; those comparator clips retain the intended words. That reversal and the failure to clear the first confession seed prevent a general template recommendation. The authored template package includes wording and structure changes, so the observed contrast does not isolate formatting alone.

Earlier results explain why seed recurrence remains central. [Group 1](h3-multistyle-group1-results.md) supplied an A phone caption failure; it did not recur in [Group 2](h3-caption-group2-results.md), and the Group 4 phone samples were also clear. Group 2's B/C confession clips were all captioned, and [Group 3](h3-caption-group3-results.md) added caption-positive A outputs on those selected seeds. The evidence supports keeping A as a working baseline while testing a caption intervention. It does not establish a caption-immune configuration or justify pooling these selected studies into global rates.

Word survival remains a validity check for a caption intervention: the intended clauses were present in fixed, unhinted audio AI transcripts for the two clear template gains. Three other clear clips had no speech detected and were excluded from dialogue-preserving clear counts. These checks do not establish overall intelligibility, absence of all gibberish, speaker attribution, acting or synchronization. No speech-quality repair is part of this review.

**Recommended next decision:** prioritize the exact full template package against current full for the caption question. The useful next question is whether the confession benefit recurs on independent matched seeds with the intended words retained. Reusing retained caption-positive Group 3 controls could instead support a narrower selected-case rescue question; that should be distinguished from prospective fresh-seed recurrence before choosing the next group's size. The existing evidence gives no reason to repeat all four prompt versions as the default next comparison. No next group, execution plan or Goal is started by this review.
