## Intended result and decision

Establish high practical confidence that replacing generic anti-text prompt boilerplate with faithful positive picture/audio descriptions materially reduces unwanted H3 subtitles without sacrificing authored Mandarin or intentionally visible writing. The evidence must go beyond the two successful exploratory shots and include non-regression and fresh-scene transfer.

This is an output-quality investigation, not a guarantee of subtitle-free H3, a 99% population-reliability claim, an inference about a particular token's internal mechanism, or a new mandatory media-review gate. The 13 historical clips were selected from one story and are not a random population sample. Quality judgments remain optional in the product under ADR 0226. Any production skill/compiler change, activation or default-quality decision is a separate implementation scope coordinated with the director. Q1 improvement is the principal hypothesis; a small Q8 non-regression check is required because the current production skill runs on Q8. A Q1-only success cannot establish the safety of changing Q8-facing compiler wording.
