# Paired independent AI content assessment

This companion follows only the opaque groupings in pairs.json. The six original score sheets and assessment.md remain frozen. No scores, thresholds or pooled gate are added. “Left” and “right” denote grouping positions alone. Requested settings remain GPT-6.1 Sol / High; effective host settings are unverified. This is AI assessment, not owner personal acceptance.

The independent reading inspected all four canonical artifacts and all six complete routes per sample. This comparison reuses that reading, verifies selected dialogue/actions/visible-text entries against the canonical JSON again, and traces scene/shot references to verify route durations and shot counts. Numeric timings below are edit budgets, not additional quality scores. JSON pointers are zero-based and belong to the named sample. Repeated instructions in bible, beats and compositions are not automatically repeated presented action. No generated media, performed audio, runtime choice display or finished text composite was inspected.

## pair-1

Left: review-1f49227747353412799b1f4bc0003e8e.
Right: review-5e07fe8ccda84115e0c10148cd391a2e.

**Supported preference: left for dramatic content; right for simplicity and editing economy.** Left supplies an observable test of the mysterious letter and makes later choices about the protagonist’s relation to it. Right offers atmosphere and distinct object dispositions, but decisive information remains asserted rather than shared with the viewer. Neither fully earns the need to surrender the bell.

- **Narrative clarity — prefer left.** “寄给过去的我。” and “天光触水，木桩会断。” (left /sceneBeats/dialogueCues/1/text and /2/text) supply a claim and a concrete prediction. The intact-post image precedes its break (left /storyboard/shots/4, /11, /19), so viewers understand why the letter gains authority. Right asserts recognition of a future self’s signature (/sceneBeats/beats/1/visibleEvent) without supplying signature/date details. “船钟，封港线……” (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/1/text) names objects without their causal relation. “后面的，还没写完。” (/4/text) suggests an open future, but the sparse earlier letter content weakens that payoff. Left’s past-image callback is a clearer closure, though it suggests rather than explains time travel.

- **Branch causality — prefer left, with limits.** Surrender versus retention persists as shore versus onboard bell and distant versus nearby sound (left /storyGraph/edges/1/stateEffects/sourceMapConsequence and /edges/2/stateEffects/sourceMapConsequence). The departure ending presses only an onboard bell quiet, or hears the shore bell without reaching for it (/storyboard/shots/33/action; shot node-8171d8dd-ac17-593f-bedd-471d0df7bc9d-s1-c5). Right’s first inspections change the clue shown, but the ordinary echo does not demonstrate a traversable gap (/sceneBeats/beats/13–14; /storyboard/shots/8). Left preserves consequence better, but both bell options still traverse open water, witness the same break and allow every ending; its first dilemma is not strongly consequential. Right’s final physical dispositions differ, but why to prefer each remains less developed.

- **Continuity — prefer left.** Its join retains bell location and common letter/time/place (/storyGraph/joinContracts/0/reconciliation). The ending callback explicitly reuses past footage rather than moving the surrendered bell back onboard (/sceneBeats/beats/45/visibleEvent; /storyboard/shots/40/action). Fold lines, missing corner and damp edge preserve the same letter through destructive or recycled states (/storyBible/props/0). Right mostly tracks its objects, but it puts the letter away and approaches the bell, then begins the inspection by approaching with the letter already unfolded (/sceneBeats/beats/5–6), a small repeated approach/prop reset. “交出船钟” becomes leaving it ashore without a recipient (/storyGraph/edges/6/choiceText; /sceneBeats/beats/17/visibleEvent). These ambiguities are not fatal impossibilities.

- **Performance readability and natural Chinese — prefer left’s intentions, with mixed phrasing.** “我是谁？”, “回去认认。” and “原来是我。” (left /sceneBeats/dialogueCues/0, /5, /8) attach short lines to different actions and mental turns. “回去认认” is colloquial but elliptical; it needs the return-to-berth image to supply its object. Right’s “我怎么什么都不记得了？” (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/0) is natural but generic exposition. Its “这回，我自己等天亮。” (/5) is a strong playable assertion of agency. Both use restrained soliloquy. Left’s advantage comes from an actual setup and reversal, not merely fewer words.

- **Shot language — prefer left’s staging; right has economy.** Left establishes shore-left/outlet-right geography and an identifiable post, then answers the prediction with separate break coverage (/storyBible/locations/0–1; /storyboard/shots/4, /11). The same-letter replay explicitly crosses time (/storyboard/shots/40; shot node-82343dae-c776-5286-918d-845134e13938-s1-c5). Right’s extreme close-up of incomplete matching marks is good (/storyboard/shots/5), and the wide fog shots suit its tone. However, landing, unfolding and discovering the blank share one medium shot (/14), leaving the insert and time ellipsis vague. Left’s conditional ending compositions still require selection of the established incoming state before production; this reading does not prove a runtime selected it correctly.

- **Pacing and edit cost — prefer right’s practical envelope.** Right routes have ten shots in 42 or 44 seconds; left has twenty to twenty-two shots in 57 or 60. These are complete-route counts, not total inventories. Right leaves more room for a bell decay, look or landing ellipsis, though its crossing remains compressed. Left devotes many cuts to moving/storing/retrieving props. Separate paper-release inserts (/storyboard/shots/29–31), a line, conditional bell response and departure elaborate one act of release. Reverse-water compositing and a replay add meaning and production cost. Left’s coherence advantage does not prove a comfortable contemplative minute.

Comparative uncertainty: The editing preference for right does not reverse the dramatic preference for left. Frozen scores are broad categories; a qualitative preference within the same category is not a new score. Conditional bell variants, actual break motion and performed timing remain untested.

## pair-2

Left: review-c92eeaa2e46ce4df00597720eaec527d.
Right: review-c48aa747f1e7aca73ba34f073385d30f.

**Supported preference: right for embodied character, continuity and clearer consequences; mixed on pacing.** The key/home interaction makes loss personal, and separate destination shots clarify the final distribution. Both leave the theater’s competing value thin. Left’s photograph is useful direct evidence and its simpler construction has a real economy.

- **Narrative clarity — prefer right, retaining the shared motive gap.** “门开了。这是我家？” (right /sceneBeats/dialogueCues/0/text) distinguishes practical recognition from belonging, using key, lock and threshold (/sceneBeats/beats/1–3). Left’s family looking blankly at each other is understandable, and its failed photograph recognition is concrete (/sceneBeats/beats/9–10), but its family has less individual agency. Right specifies hospital backup lighting rather than implying a guaranteed medical rescue (/sceneBeats/beats/26; /storyGraph/nodes/6/summary). Its empty theater still has no specific person or memory motivating the electrician’s sacrifice (/storyBible/locations/2/description; /storyGraph/nodes/7). Right clarifies what happens more than why theater should rival hospital.

- **Branch causality — prefer right.** The investigations demonstrate pattern matching without a harmful experiment versus a personal loss persisting as light fades (right /sceneBeats/beats/8–23). Final sequences separately preserve supported and unsupported destinations and the new loss; refusal retains the old damage without causing another (/sceneBeats/beats/24–47). Left’s visit-to-join inference moves from one family to certainty about a universal rule more quickly (/storyGraph/joinContracts/0/reconciliation). Both keep all final options available. Right’s record-route join still claims “熄灯不能撤销损失” (/storyGraph/nodes/4/summary) without that route showing the resident branch’s dimming observation. - **Continuity — prefer right.** Fixed cabinet/near-door/far-door geography and separate stable destination interiors supply a clear map (/storyBible/locations/0–2). Withheld reserve stays low while used reserve reaches exhaustion; the original resident keeps her key and does not recover (/sceneBeats/beats/25, /33, /40–42). Left combines “另一处住户门前” with the electrician receiving new loss information in a single S01 shot without specifying screen or intercut (/storyboard/shots/13, shot node-d748eee0-2a20-5c7d-a245-a95cd5bbe29f-s1-c2; /17). Right avoids that remote/local ambiguity. Its incoming branches do have different ambient brightness, so matching into the shared ending remains an edit concern.

- **Performance readability and natural Chinese — prefer right, with some thematic stiffness.** “钥匙是我的。里面……不认得。” (right /sceneBeats/dialogueCues/6/text) is idiomatic hesitant speech tied to a visible object; its 5.5-second shot leaves room (/storyboard/shots/13, shot node-46afee8a-7967-50b4-bb64-357e500ef48f-s1-c2). “里面有认得的吗？” (/5) lets the electrician address her. Left’s “我送去了医院。” / “我送去了剧院。” (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/3–4) are intelligible with electricity as the omitted object, but mostly report the visible choice. Right’s “两边都没电，是我断的。” (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/12) gives responsibility a more active form. “这边的黑，我也得认。” (/8) is poetic but sounds authored; “灯亮了，人没想起来。” (/10) states the theme more than advancing conversation. Right’s clearest speech improvement is the resident exchange.

- **Shot language — prefer right overall; left directly shows the additional victim.** Right separates hospital, theater and slope into readable inserts (/storyboard/shots/19–23, /26–30) and provides explicit hand/gauge coverage. Left’s family and photograph give direct evidence of damaged recognition. Actual VO placement was not heard.

- **Pacing and edit cost — mixed.** Left uses twelve shots in 45–46 seconds; right uses nineteen in 57–59. Right’s extra time often buys emotional specificity, particularly the key exchange. It also repeats board/gauge looks, turns back to controls and spoken responsibility after visible consequences (/storyboard/shots/3–11; /22, /29, /36). Left is shorter and cheaper but must resolve the combined remote/local shot. Right adds destination assets and inserts, although keeping them empty makes them easier to reuse. Repetition across mutually exclusive endings is not repetition within a route, and boilerplate is not playback. A near-minute budget is not measured comfortable pacing.

Comparative uncertainty: Right’s advantage is strongest in the person/key/home interaction. Its unshown new household loss, thin theater motive and absent accepted board text remain limitations. No original score or fatal flag was changed.

## pair-3

Left: review-bc322f8ecff543c6d7ca701eed6bbe3e.
Right: review-8883d25c11babeff4ae32ff8aa9178e1.

**Supported preference: left for dramatic design and differentiated outcomes; right for lower reading and editing load.** Left supplies a relationship cycle and limits what each delivered message repairs. Its dense minute and partly editorial on-screen wording need substantial work. Right communicates a simpler house dilemma, but its one-letter restriction is unconvincing at a shared destination and its promised collateral consequences are not staged.

- **Narrative clarity — prefer left’s emotional account, with an accessibility cost.** The same exclusive old act is claimed by three living writers (left /storyboard/shots/1/visibleTexts/0/text, /2/visibleTexts/0/text, /3/visibleTexts/0/text). Father→daughter→nurse→father addresses explain whom each confession protects (/sceneBeats/beats/11, /15). “爸，你是让我走。” (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/4/text) pays off an emotional question. Right plainly voices the three instructions in its opening (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/0–2), easier to parse immediately, but dismisses differing dates with “日子不同，还不能作准。” (/4) without explaining why the latest cancellation might fail. Left is clearer once understood, not automatically easier on a first viewing with three new names and a past event.

- **Branch causality — prefer left, but its access constraint is incomplete.** Left’s deliveries change different people and preserve losses elsewhere. The nurse accompanies the daughter without delivering the absent father’s reassurance: “爸还是不肯让我走。” receives “我陪你。” (left /sceneBeats/dialogueCues/7–8; /sceneBeats/beats/33–38). The keeper’s actual arrival changes the nurse’s company in another ending (/sceneBeats/beats/43–45). Right keeps all three letters at the same recipient’s door while the courier remains beside her (/sceneBeats/beats/20–40); a snowbound road does not explain withholding the next sheet. Left’s separate destinations and warning are a better basis. However, its nurse walks clinic→shelter while the courier with the father’s original stays at the clinic (/sceneBeats/beats/32–33). The one-way rule bars returning to the postal road (/storyboard/shots/8/visibleTexts/0/text), not following that same outbound local path to the daughter. The restriction is more developed in left but still needs a specific timing/access reason.

- **Continuity — prefer left.** Delivered originals change hands, correct remaining letters stay in the bag, recipients do not suddenly know unseen messages, and altered company has an explicit cause (left /sceneBeats/beats/28, /32–38, /42–48). Conflicting exclusive confessions are unreliable testimony, not contradictions in the narrative’s facts. Right tracks its remaining letters and key handover (/sceneBeats/beats/30–33), but its visit route reaches the correct recipient, withholds every letter, leaves for the fork and later returns as closure approaches (/10–14). This can physically happen, yet the absent motivation strains the deadline. Left’s detailed route prose still cannot substitute for a demonstrated closure clock.

- **Performance readability and natural Chinese — prefer left.** “我去陪她。”, “我陪你。” and “她也认了？” (left /sceneBeats/dialogueCues/6, /8, /9) give distinct intentions: commit, reassure, recognize shared self-blame. They are natural short lines whose meaning depends on the recipient. “爸，你是让我走。” (/4) gives a believable realization. Right’s “路要封了，我得走。” (/sceneBeats/dialogueCues/3/text) is also natural and efficient. Its “那我先留下。” versus “那就先不动这屋子。” (/5, /7) produces a smaller difference in intention than left’s different relationships and actions. Left’s “不能凭这三封信定罪。” (/2, /3) is formal and explanatory; the strongest speech is interpersonal. Some supplied on-screen prose is considerably less natural than the dialogue.

- **Shot language — prefer left’s consequence coverage, while crediting right’s simple anchors.** Left links reading with face/turn/leave actions and then shows other locations’ resulting states. The station window and empty boarding position distinguish continuing isolation from departure (/storyboard/shots/20–27, /29–36, /38–44). Right’s key handover is a specific image (/storyboard/shots/23), and its bag remains legible. But its “运行时呈现节拍” entries are purpose-only (/sceneBeats/beats/25, /32, /39), linked to shots showing unsent sheets rather than missed arrangements (/storyboard/shots/18, /24, /30). Left shows more actual changed destinies; right asks the bag to carry consequences it does not demonstrate. Left pays for the better coverage with more locations and cuts.

- **Pacing and edit cost — prefer right’s envelope; left has a substantial unresolved load.** Right has sixteen shots in 55 seconds; left has twenty-two or twenty-three in 60. Left’s three confession texts each receive three seconds (/storyboard/shots/1–3), then the investigation repeats both propositions in two-second inserts (/9–10 or /14–15). Folding, re-enveloping and bagging all three is budgeted as two seconds (/12 or /17). Four-second delivery shots combine receipt, unfolding, reading, reaction and speech (/29, /38). These are actual text/action collisions, not an assumption that longer JSON means slower drama. Visible-text fields include editorial descriptions: “正文对同一刻、同一只闸柄声称…” (/2/visibleTexts/0/text) and “警示文字交代跨支路邮路将封…” (/8/visibleTexts/0/text). These are intelligible authoring instructions, not natural finished letter/sign language as supplied. Right carries its simpler conflict mostly through short speech and luggage/key actions, reducing reading load. It still overpacks the opening extraction/unfolding and fragments small ending actions. Its economy does not repair the causal restriction or unshown collateral losses.

Comparative uncertainty: Left’s emotional differentiation supports a content preference, not a claim that its current minute is watchable. The clinic-to-shelter route makes the courier’s stopping reason especially salient, but does not establish a new fatal contradiction or justify changing a frozen score. Typography, transit/closure times and performance could materially change the practical result. Right’s easier immediate parsing does not make its selective delivery motivated.
