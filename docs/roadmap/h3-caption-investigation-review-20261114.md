## Judgment and practical production guidance

The experiments answered their agreed local questions, including negative results. They did not deliver a reliable caption remedy or establish a globally best model/backend. Completion of technical execution, review and preservation is distinct from caption prevention and creative acceptance.

Continue to use the existing native H3 production path and story-required characters. Keep the current working A configuration as the research baseline. The results supply no caption-based reason to migrate production to BF16 or vLLM-Omni, and no automatic change to a production prompt default. The tested full creator-template package is a tentative option on one scene, with failures retained alongside its gains.

Story character identity, appearance and required dialogue are fixed creative requirements. Wenjun reports never having seen this defect with a Western face in the earlier videos. That is a useful owner observation, not a measured face-stratified result. The generic phone-reference prompt did not establish a verified Western-face counterexample. The study did not isolate appearance, spoken language and scene, and proving such an association would not itself solve the production problem. Do not optimize away story characters or turn that observation into a separate appearance study without a new decision.

The existing production instruction for caption-only retries remains applicable where already authorized: use a fresh seed with unchanged prompts and references unless a separate defect warrants an input change; record the source, outcome and reason. This retrospective authorizes no new submission or retry. Research diagnostics, mandatory per-clip quality review and audio qualification are not new production workflow requirements.

## Measurement, validity and remaining unknowns

Conventional screen-space dialogue subtitle rows, including garbled/translated rows, counted as caption-present. Physical lettering, UI panels and speech bubbles were recorded separately in later groups. All-frame fixed visual judgments were made before condition/seed/history disclosure; source-neutral guide/word disclosure followed raw observations. A missing or unreviewed output never counted as clear. Do not infer a new clean judgment from an OCR pass or from lack of text transcription.

Groups 1–3 did not have qualified speech/synchronization evidence. Groups 4 and 5 used the pinned local Qwen audio-only path with spoken and zero-waveform controls, no picture or expected words in the actual audio observation, fixed raw responses, then fresh Terra source-word comparison. Genuine homophones such as 路/陆 and 浩/昊 were accepted; punctuation variation was immaterial.

Group 4 verified intended clauses for **45/48** clips. Three visually clear masked clips had no speech detected (CF on rounds 1 and 2; TF on round 3), leaving **38 clear clips with verified words**. Group 5 verified intended clauses for **16/16** clips. These are qualified AI word observations, not human listening or a general intelligibility/acting/voice-identity grade. Qwen's onset/end guesses sometimes exceeded actual duration; they were retained and unused. Timing, synchronization and comprehensive speech quality remain ungraded. A clear intervention with missing/uncertain intended words did not count as a dialogue-preserving gain.

All five groups completed native request/history binding, complete AV decode, resource release, agent-operated functional browser playback and service restoration. Their reviews reused exact originals; known pre-model faults were repaired on the same frozen cell after reconciliation. No quality reroll was silently substituted. Historical preservation passed at closeout; the owner's later reduced-retention decision intentionally retires most media and derived proof, without changing the recorded outcomes.
