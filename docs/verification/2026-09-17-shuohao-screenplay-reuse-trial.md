turn this bounded terminal route into a reviewable screenplay with useful
deterministic checks and a rendered report. The single draft is 30.0 seconds,
with nine beats (four actions and five attributed dialogue lines). It realizes

feasibility evidence, not causal superiority over Qwen or approval of an
adapter.

The final upstream validation and checkup fail exactly two gates: this terminal
excerpt has no source-grounded opening hook/cliff and therefore no `hookBeat`.
Those fields remain empty; no hook or cliff was invented. The Markdown and HTML
reports were rendered for review. The checkup, rather than a rendered label, is
the authoritative deterministic result.

## Review and comparison boundary

The retained Plotloom ending candidate is artifact
`174b27c4-e95a-4ff7-b9cf-6c8c6f250001` from the same prompt attempt. Its
validator accepted a 30-second Scene Beats fragment, but its second beat says
the siblings repair the watch and its action says the second hand starts again,
despite the direct-entry contract already setting the watch to `已修复`. The
corrected Shuohao/Terra excerpt instead begins with an already repaired watch
and prepares it for customer delivery. That is evidence that this draft obeys
the corrected source projection. It is not causal-superiority evidence: the
retained candidate used the Qwen runtime and Plotloom fragment contract,
whereas this trial uses an attended native Terra High author following Shuohao
instructions.

An independent attended, read-only Terra review inspected the source artifact,
frozen projection, final script/render, and deterministic output. It confirmed
that the draft does not re-repair/restart the watch, infer a physical shop repair
or sign, or confuse shop sale with watch delivery. It found a concrete choice
and consequences, resolved dialogue attribution in the rendered script, and
adequate audiovisual anchors for this compact excerpt (contract tearing, watch
packet, counter/natural light, and a mutual look). It calls the literal location
state label weakly audiovisual as a creative concern, not a deterministic
failure. It also records a provenance variance: the retained Plotloom prompt
had a two-dialogue-cue capacity while the Shuohao draft has five lines; the
upstream Shuohao checkup does not test that variance. Neither point supports a
claim of full-episode acceptance or creative approval.

## Reuse boundary

**Do not resume the custom two-phase integration.** A separately authorized,
small adapter investigation may evaluate screenplay authoring for a terminal
or other bounded linear excerpt as well as a complete episode, but must preserve
the excerpt's truthful missing-hook/cliff state rather than manufacture it.

| Category | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Directly reusable | Outline-to-seed projection, structured action/dialogue screenplay shape, duration/line/speaker/action/reference gates, Markdown/HTML reviewer report, and role-oriented line book. |
| Thin adapter needed | Plotloom-ID-to-outline/art/cast projection; explicit linear-excerpt mode that reports rather than falsely satisfies hook/cliff gates; a report that does not imply empty-hook fulfillment; tracked source attribution and output quarantine. |
| Interactive-only gap | Graph direct-entry/join contracts, typed state validation, canonical IDs/binding, stage staleness, route selection, and Plotloom's storyboard/audio ownership remain Plotloom responsibilities. |
| Likely removable complexity if an adapter is accepted | The experimental compact-semantic-to-full-fragment expansion path may be unnecessary for screenplay authoring. This is not authorization to remove it or any current production behavior. |

The screenplay's compactness argues against treating a passing structural shape
as a quality gate. Any future complete-episode evaluation would need actual
source-grounded hook and ending material plus separately reviewed audiovisual
consequence; it must not fill those gaps by invention. That future requirement
does not invalidate bounded-excerpt reuse investigation.
