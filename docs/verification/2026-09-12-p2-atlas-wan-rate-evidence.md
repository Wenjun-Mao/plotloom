# P2 Atlas Wan rate evidence

Captured 2026-09-12 before the first paid request. AtlasCloud's public model
page for `alibaba/wan-3.0/image-to-video` states **$0.04 per generated second**.
It does not publish a resolution-tier distinction on that page; audio is shown
at the same stated rate. A five-second indicative estimate is $0.20 and the
100 requested-second local allowance is $4.00 before provider billing
differences. This is rate evidence, not a billing receipt or dollar cap.

The P2 runtime enforces the independent, conservative requested-seconds ledger:
100 seconds may be reserved once across projects and restarts, and a dispatch
claim remains counted after a timeout, rejection, unknown outcome, or refund.

Source: [AtlasCloud Wan 3.0 image-to-video model page](https://www.atlascloud.ai/models/alibaba/wan-3.0/image-to-video)
